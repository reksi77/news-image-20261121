Magnitude 6.7 quake shakes Peru’s Andes

A tanker sailing through the Strait of Hormuz was hit on its stern by an unidentified projectile 11 nautical miles north of Khasab, the UK maritime agency UKMTO said.

SOURCE: REUTERS / UKMTO
